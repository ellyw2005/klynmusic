About the Song

Newly emerging independent artist Klyn has dropped "That's Not Who We Are." It speaks to an audience that feels trapped by expectations but still has a quiet fire inside them. It's emotional without being melodramatic — cathartic, honest, and subtly rebellious.

About the Artist

Born in Shanghai and now based in New York, Klyn crafts emotionally charged songs that speak to the quiet struggles and silent revolutions within us all.

Blending dark pop, indie pop, jazz, and alternative influences, her music feels both cinematic and deeply personal — a place where fear, hope, and rebellion coexist.

With a voice that carries both fragility and strength, she invites listeners to step beyond expectations and reclaim their truth. For anyone who's ever felt trapped between worlds or words, Klyn offers a sound that says: that's not who we are.
